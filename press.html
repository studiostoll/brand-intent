Short texts and facts about Brand Intent, for articles and interviews. Quote freely. For anything else, write to christophe@stoll.studio.

In one sentence

Brand Intent is an open file format that makes the reasoning behind a brand readable for people and AI agents: nine plain-text file types for identity, visual expression, formats, content purposes, compositions, stories, and the facts about places, time and people.

In one paragraph

Design tokens capture decisions and brand guidelines capture rules, but neither captures intent: why this typeface, why never that word, why this audience first. Brand Intent, written by the Hamburg designer Christophe Stoll, gives that reasoning a structure concrete enough to test against. A brand is described in nine plain-text file types: an identity with voice rules an agent can check, a brand file with colours, typography and photo treatment, formats for each platform, purposes that say what a post is for, compositions that place its parts, stories for what the brand posts page by page, and three fact files: places with address and hours, the brand's time with seasons, week and day, and people with what they agreed to. The format is open and MIT licensed, with a parser, a VS Code extension and a commented example brand. Stoll uses it with clients in Brand Atelier, a tool that renders posts from these files and keeps AI-written text inside the rules the brand authored.

About the author

Christophe Stoll is an interaction designer in Hamburg. He has designed digital products and the design systems behind them for more than twenty-five years, co-founded precious design studio, and teaches interaction design at HAWK Hildesheim. With Studio Stoll he works as designer and consultant for brands, and Brand Intent and Brand Atelier are the tools that came out of that work.
