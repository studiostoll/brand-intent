01 / The problem

The reasoning behind a brand is rarely written down.

Brand teams write vision, mission and values, then jump to the visual identity. The reasoning in between (why this typeface, why never that word, why this audience first) lives in someone’s head, or on page 47 of the brand book. Guidelines stay fuzzy: “be authentic”, “use a warm tone”. Two designers read the same line and make two different things, both arguably on brand.

The problem is older than AI. AI made it fast. A designer fills fuzzy guidelines with taste and context. An agent has neither, and falls back on the average. Brand Intent gives the reasoning a structure concrete enough to test against, written so that a person, a parser and an agent read the same file the same way.

02 / The framework

Eleven file types in three groups: what a brand says once, what it posts, and what is true about it.

Each file type answers one question. No file repeats what an earlier one settled: a purpose narrows the identity, it doesn’t restate it. So the reasoning holds while the expression changes.

03 / The files

Eleven file types. A few lines from each.

All excerpts come from Krume, the commented example bakery that ships with the package. Each heading opens the whole file.

01

Identity krume.identity

The strategic core: essence, promise, positioning, narratives. Voice with checkable always and never rules, and instead pairs that teach the rewrite. Values as behaviour, imagery rules, goals, and the review questions asked before anything goes out. Nothing visual. 

voice
  register:  informal, Du
  persona:   The baker behind the counter: direct, dry, proud of the craft
  never:
    - lifestyle language (curated, hand-picked, moment of indulgence)
    - "artisan" or "artisanal": we say craft
  instead:
    - "the finest ingredients" -> Tipo 1 and rye 1150 from the mill in Dachau
    - "baked with love" -> eighteen hours of fermentation
    - "fresh from the oven" -> on the shelf since six

review
  copy:
    - Does the text name the bread, the flour, the weight or the fermentation time?
    - Does it sound like the baker behind the counter?
  image:
    - Does the picture show a real step of the work or real customers?
    - Is the bread food, not decoration?
02

Brand krume.brand

The expression: colours and themes, fonts and typography styles, spacing, dividers, badges, icons. And the brand’s place in the world: a location for sunrise and weather, a camera, framing modules for photos, a photo-look. The photo library is the photos/ folder: a file’s name is its id, its folders are the sections. Every value traces back to a sentence in the identity. 

brand-colors
  primary:   #2C1810   # dark roast: a well-baked crust
  accent1:   #C8A47E   # crust: dividers and highlights
  white:     #FAF6F0   # flour: not pure white, slightly warm

theme Shop
  background:   $white
  text-primary: $primary
  divider:      $accent1

font primary
  name:   Hanken Grotesk
  source: google

typography headline
  weight:     700
  size:       6
  lineHeight: 1.15
03

Format instagram-4-5-feed-portrait.format

The canvas: platform, size in pixels or millimetres, grid and three safe zones. Social, print and signage. Its one brand decision: which purposes belong here. 

size:     1080 1350
grid:     5 4           # 5 rows × 4 columns
crop:     0 34 0 34     # px - profile grid crops top/bottom
comfort:  40            # px - safe reading margin
purposes: daily-bread, seasonal-special, baking-tip
04

Purpose daily-bread.purpose

What one kind of post is for. Semantic slots (primary, secondary, detail, meta, label, cta, credit), each with a style, a colour and a length. A scope and context that narrow the identity for this kind of post. And examples: whole pages, one topic each, the shape AI writing has to match. 

context
  primary is the bread name, 1-3 words (e.g. "Rye loaf").
  secondary is the weight and price ("750 g · 5,80 €").
  meta is one sentence: what makes this bread specific today.
  Never describe the bread as "delicious" or "special" -
  state a fact (flour type, fermentation time, seasonal ingredient).

example
  topic: Butter stollen, baked from the first Sunday of Advent
  when:  advent
  label:     Advent only
  primary:   Butter stollen
  secondary: 1 kg · €24
  meta:      Butter from Brandenburg, matured for three weeks. On the shelf from 27 November.
05

Composition simple-stack.composition

Where the slots go. Grid mode places them in rows and columns; flow mode stacks them in panels with sticky bars, spacers and dividers. Photos and illustrations too. Nothing brand-specific, so one composition serves any brand. 

sticky: bottom s
  | logo right

flow: panel bottom-left 2/3 1/1
  | spacer-auto
  | label left
  | primary left
  | divider-line
  | secondary left
  | spacer-s
  | meta left
  | spacer-auto
06

Story today.story

What the brand posts, page by page. Each page names a purpose and a composition. A slot is fixed, open, or a variable: {weekday}, {sunrise}, {weather}, {closes}, {days-until}, {person}. A variable without a value is not guessed: no forecast, or a closed day, and the post doesn’t go out. Page lists repeat a page as often as the week needs. 

id:     today
format: 4:5
place:  weserstrasse

page
  purpose:     daily-bread
  composition: editorial
  label:       {weekday}
  meta:        Light since {sunrise}, oven on since four. Open until {closes}, what’s gone is gone.
07

Time krume.time

The brand’s rhythm: its founding, its seasons, the windows a seasonal post fits, and its day. A story names a window or a season with when: and only comes up in season. Occasions are works. 

founded: 2019-05-04

season winter
  label: Winter
  from:  12-01

windows
  rhubarb: 04-15..06-24
  advent:  11-27..12-24

daily
  oven:  04:00  Oven on
  shelf: 06:00  Everything on the shelf
08

Place weserstrasse.place

A site the posts speak about: a shop, a café, an information office. Address, coordinates, a context line, capacity and weekly hours. A day not named is closed. 

address:   Weserstraße 17, 12045 Berlin
context:   Former copy shop with one counter and three breads a day. Regulars at seven, sold out by noon on Saturdays.

hours
  tue-fri: 07:00-14:00
  sat:     07:00-13:00
  sun:     closed
  mon:     closed

capacity
  indoor:  6
  outdoor: 8
09

Person baker.person

One person the posts name, quote or show. A name or a role, their reason, their photos, their own words, and the date they agreed. The file is the consent: no name in the file, no name in the post. Withdrawing a person is deleting one file. 

id:     baker
role:   Baker
since:  2019
reason: Quit her agency job and turned the copy shop into a bakery.
consent: 2019-05-04  # no name: she is "the baker" in every post

quote
  What’s gone is gone. Tomorrow there’s more.
10

Group drax-mill.group

Several people under one name: a band, a team, a company, a family. Name, role, region, members, photos and the words it published as one. A group is public, so the file is no consent; its people have their own person files. 

id:    drax-mill
name:  Drax mill
role:  The mill
where: Dachau

quote
  Stone-ground, nothing taken out, nothing put in.
11

Work wheat-blend.work

What the brand puts out or puts on: a product, a release, a class, an event. Title and kind in the brand’s own words, who it is by, the price, the facts a post may state, what critics wrote about it word for word, and its date: a release day with a countdown, or the days it comes round. A series gives one item per day or week, never twice, and a page can take its colour from the day’s item. 

id:    wheat-blend
title: Wheat blend
kind:  bread
days:  sat
price: 500 g · €4.20
facts:
  Tipo 1 from the Drax mill, 72 % hydration. Sold out by ten.

04 / How it is used

With me, or without me.

Brand Atelier, with me

Brand Atelier is the tool I run with clients. We author the files together; Atelier renders posts from them, writes text inside the rules, and follows the brand’s own review, scheduling and publishing. It is in live testing with a few brands.

The files are the easy part. The work is keeping them true over years, extending the language when a brand needs to say something new, and fitting it into how a team decides. I do that with each brand myself, so Brand Atelier is invite only for now.

Write to me

The format, without me

The language is open and MIT licensed, with spec, parser, examples and VS Code extension. Write your own brand, point your agent at it, build a renderer, or take only the vocabulary.

What I want back is feedback: what you couldn’t say with it, what a parser should have refused, which rule is wrong for your brand. Open an issue, or write.

05 / Try it

Ten minutes with the example brand.

1. Install the VS Code extension

Syntax highlighting, completion, hover docs and validation for all eleven file types.

extinstall studiostoll.brand-intent-lang

Or install from the VS Code Marketplace.

2. Run init

Copies the Krume example into your project and installs two skills into your AI agent: /brand-intent to explore, author, generate and validate, and /brief to export a portable markdown brief.

$npx brand-intent init

Works with: Claude Code, Cursor, Gemini CLI, VS Code Copilot, Codex CLI

3. Explore, then write your own

Explore (/brand-intent example). Your agent walks you through the Krume files: why the identity says what it says, how the brand derives from it, what a purpose adds and what it may not.

Generate and validate (/brand-intent daily-bread). Content for a purpose, checked against the voice rules, the pillars, the anti-audiences and the review questions.

Check (npx brand-intent check). Every file in the folder parsed, and the files checked against each other: each person, work, place and photo a file names exists, and both sides agree.

Onboard (/brand-intent onboard). A guided interview drafts your identity, brand and a first purpose: a draft to argue with.

Parse. The package exports the TypeScript parser for all eleven types, so your own tooling can read the files.

A work keeps what critics wrote: a review block holds their words verbatim, credited with by:. brand-intent check parses a brand folder and checks its files against each other, the same command for an agent, a repo’s CI and an app.

The photo library is the photos/ folder: drop a file in and it is a photo, its name the id, its folders the sections, two levels deep. A photo block only adds facts; src: and section: are gone. brand-intent photos writes the file list.

A work can be a series: its items come one per day or per week, never twice, with {item}, {item-name} and {item-note}. A story page takes its background from a hex or from the day’s item.
